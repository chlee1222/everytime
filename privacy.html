LastWise

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

In short: LastWise does not collect any data about you. Everything you track stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

The trackers you create, the dates you log, and your settings (for example, the age you enter for long-term projections) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server, and we cannot see, access or recover your data.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, your app data is included in that backup in the same way as other apps' data. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

No account, no analytics, no ads

You don't sign up or sign in. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and it does not use any third-party service that collects data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone and delivered by iOS. Nothing about them is sent to a server. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Settings app.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether your subscription is active; Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

LastWise is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting an entry in the app removes it from everything the app shows; deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Copies in your own device backups are managed through your iCloud or computer settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy and the App Store's privacy information will say so before that happens.

Support

Everytime remembers when you last did the things that are easy to forget — the dentist, the sheets, an oil change, a visit home — and reminds you when they come round.

Need help? Email everytimeapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Everytime. Cancel at least a day before your free week or billing period ends to avoid being charged. Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so we can't cancel them for you.

How does the free week work?

The yearly plan starts with 7 days free. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and if notifications are on, the app reminds you two days before. Cancel any time before then and you pay nothing. Each Apple ID can use the free trial once.

I already subscribed — how do I get access back?

On the plans screen, tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Where is my data? Will I lose it if I change phones?

Everything stays on your iPhone — there's no account. Your trackers are included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, so restoring a new phone from that backup brings them with you. Deleting the app deletes its data.

When do notifications arrive?

At most one a day, at 9:00 in the morning, and only on days when a reminder comes round. Trackers without a reminder never send notifications. You can turn them off in the Settings app under Notifications.
